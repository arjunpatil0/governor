**4. dbh_llama32_1b_S1_m0.3 (68.9 tok/s, 45.2 W, aligned) vs db_llama32_1b_S1_m0.3 (47.0 tok/s, 59.5 W, unaligned): a run-to-run mode flip of d'B, not a bug.** Facts from gov_log and metrics: in T3b d'B left the aligned start mode at t = 1.9 s (its first possible switch) for unaligned pacing and stayed there: commanded clock at the 2460 MHz ceiling (240 clamp events), duty 0.2-0.6, guard 0, median token 6.5 ms, counter power 59.5 W, 47.0 tok/s. In T3c it never switched: aligned pacing, 8 clock changes (a dip to 1500 MHz at 4 s, 1800 MHz from 15 s), duty 0.8, guard about 1 ms, median token 4.9 ms, 45.2 W, 68.9 tok/s (+46% at 14 W less), 0% miss in both. The first actions of the two runs already differed (T3b: duty 0.05, guard 6.0 ms; T3c: duty 0.25, guard 3.85 ms), i.e. the first observations differed, and the hybrid decides the pacing mode from the predicted tok/s of its two plans with only 2 consecutive 1 s checks (the other mode must predict 1.2x + 2 tok/s more), when its models have about 20 ticks of data. The "token-aware threshold" is c'B's (SwitchingPIController); d'B has no threshold. Why the first actions differed is not determined (hypothesis: start-up state of the background / first latencies; untested). Across the 6 repeated d'B cells at margin 0.3 this is the only flip (5 of 6 repeated cells ended in the same mode: all other 1B/3B/Phi cells at margins 0.2-0.4 end unaligned), and the 1B is not in the evaluation (3B and the held-out 8B). MEANING FOR THE EVALUATION: d'B's outcome can be bimodal where both modes are viable (here +46% tok/s with a different power); with 10 blocks per cell this will show as block-to-block variance and must be reported with the final pacing mode per cell; a settle period before the first switch would be a new controller version and is not done (no bug).

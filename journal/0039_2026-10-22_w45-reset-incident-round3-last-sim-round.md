**Smoke incident (helper log pasted by Arjun).** Both `backend reset failed (NVMLError_Unknown)` events (18:07:35 at a client disconnect, 18:31:57 at shutdown) followed a successful RESET with no LOCK in between. The log does NOT support the simple hypothesis "resetting an unlocked clock errors": back-to-back RESETs succeeded at 18:07:47 and at 18:10:36/37 with no lock between them. Both failures came after the GPU had been idle (8 s and about 20 min); the idle-GPU hypothesis (H2) is untested. Diagnostic written, not run (needs an elevated shell and the helper stopped): experiments/w4_smoke/reset_probe.py -> data/w45_smoke/reset_probe.json (separates busy vs idle, locked vs unlocked, repeated resets). After Ctrl+C the clock showed SM 1980 MHz (unlocked) and Arjun ran -rgc/-rmc; the shutdown path did reset (the final log line is the NVML error, state unlocked) but cannot be called clean.
Fix (test-first, GREEN): the helper tracks whether it knows the clock is unlocked (after a successful reset, until the next LOCK attempt); an NVML reset error in that state is logged as "reset not needed" (not an error, no dirty state); in any other state (lock held, dirty, unknown such as start-up) it remains a real failure that is remembered and retried. The helper has no subprocess by design, so the `nvidia-smi -rgc` fallback lives in the governor: ControlActuator(fallback_reset=...) calls it (at most every 5 s) when a heartbeat answer says `OK dirty`. Caveat: the "known unlocked" shortcut means a lock left by another process or a hard-killed earlier helper would be missed by a helper that issued no LOCK itself; the governor start-up reset (helper RESET + nvidia-smi -rgc/-rmc when elevated) covers that case.

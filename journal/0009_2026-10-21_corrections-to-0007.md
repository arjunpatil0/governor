## Corrections
1. **0007 item 4 (0.75 ms launch overhead "unlogged") is wrong.** It is logged: `variance_gpu_project/experiments/variance_adaptive_v2/results/calibration.json`
   has `"launch_overhead_ms": 0.751630`, with the multiplier sweep (e.g. multiplier 4000 → T_kernel 13.49 ms; chosen by "T_kernel >= 10x launch_overhead"). My search missed it because I looked for the docs, not the results folder.
   Journal 0002's "~0.75 ms, calibrated" is therefore supported. The rep-0 versus README-mean remark in 0007 item 4 stands.
2. **0007 item 3 (warp-efficiency figures "unverified") is superseded.** They reproduce from the v2 `workload.csv` files. New script
   `experiments/variance_adaptive_v2/analysis/warp_efficiency.py` (archive repo commit ba69c04, output in `warp_efficiency_output.txt`), defined as
   Σ task_cost / (32 × Σ warp maxima) per 10,000-task chunk (static-large size), unsorted versus sorted within each chunk:
   | experiment | tasks | unsorted | sorted |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | A_uniform | 1000 | 0.845 | 0.966 |
   | B_increasing_variance | 800000 | 0.456 | 0.991 |
   | C_low_high_low_bursty | 800000 | 0.462 | 0.991 |
   | D_bursty | 800000 | 0.187 | 0.970 |
   So 0.46 (B/C) and 0.19 (D) in journal 0003 are confirmed, with this definition. ARCHIVE_NOTE.md still says "not reproduced"; it is not edited here and needs its own follow-up.
3. **0003's "GPU ~14% occupied" is wrong for this GPU.** 10,000 threads / (36 SMs × 1536 threads/SM) = 18.1% (computed). 14.2% is what 46 SMs would give, so the original figure was probably computed
   for a different SM count (a guess about its origin, not verified). Use 18% for the RTX 4070 Laptop.

**Change 7: the ~5% admission mismatch is an artefact of MY analysis, not a driver bug.** The smoke-3 report joined the ring timestamps to the driver CSV by rounding both to 6 decimals. QPC ticks are 100 ns, so every timestamp whose 7th decimal is 5 is a rounding tie; 64 of 1378 rows (4.6%) found no partner, were left as NaN, and were counted as 'not admitted'. With an exact join (timestamps equal within 1 ns) all rows match and 0 of 5375 aligned starts (1379 + 1379 + 1378 + 1239 in the four aligned runs at costs 4 and 7) have (logged estimate + guard) above the time to the next arrival. The logged estimate IS the one the fit test used. The 0.5% of tokens that overran (7 per run at cost 7) were all admitted tokens that simply took longer than the estimate (up to 13.7 ms against 10.1 ms): estimation error, which the learned guard is for. Fix: match_logged() in analysis/smoke2_report.py with tests/test_smoke2_report.py (RED shown by the old rounding is not kept; the test pins exact matching on 100-ns ticks). Correction of the record: journal 0043 listed the 5% as an unexplained residual; the commit message of 1683d3c says 5301 starts, the right number is 5375.

NOT yet verified on hardware: smoke step `shift` (bg alone 40 s; expects well over 1534 tokens) is the first command of the W5a session. The exe was rebuilt (hash bb875fbc...), so it needs one warm. Do not run the build test before the sessions; it now restores the exe, but any other rebuild changes the hash.

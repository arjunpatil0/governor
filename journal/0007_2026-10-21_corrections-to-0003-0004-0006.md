## Corrections
1. **0006, Exploratory bullet:** "at ≥1200 MHz fma is also ~0.68–0.70" is wrong at 1800 MHz. data/w1_downclock_results.md:
   fma relative energy/work is 0.681 (1200), 0.697 (1500), **0.762 (1800)**; mem is 0.649, 0.687, 0.767. Correct statement:
   mem−fma differs significantly only at 600/900 MHz (Holm p 0.0049); at 1200–1800 MHz the difference is within ±0.03 and not significant.
2. **0003, "Host overhead … 5–34 ms per run":** barrier_model_output.txt gives 2–34 ms (min 2 ms: exp C static_large; max 34 ms: exp D static_matched).
   ARCHIVE_NOTE.md says 2–34; the variance_gpu_project README banner still says 5–34 and is the inconsistent one (not edited here).
3. **0003, "Warp efficiency 0.46 (B/C), 0.19 (D); GPU ~14% occupied":** stated as findings, but ARCHIVE_NOTE.md says these come from earlier analysis
   and "are not reproduced by barrier_model_output.txt; re-verify before citing." They are unverified; do not cite them in a paper.
4. **0002, "per-launch overhead (~0.75 ms, calibrated)":** the only source is docs/CODEBASE_GUIDE.md ("approximately 0.75 ms per launch"; "~0.75ms" in the
   calibration description); I found no logged measurement file. Treat as unverified. Separately, ARCHIVE_NOTE.md's table uses rep-0 kernel times
   (B: 11,793 / 2,749) while 0002 uses the README's means (11,823 / 2,757); both are right for what they cite, but they are different quantities.
5. **0004, ConsumerBench:** the "decode ~30× slower" is the paper's statement for **LiveCaptions (Whisper) decode** under greedy GPU co-running with
   ImageGen on an **RTX 6000 server** ("the decode phase in LiveCaptions runs 30× slower compared to exclusive GPU access"; 12.4× end-to-end request latency),
   not LLM decode in general; the paper gives no numeric slowdown for the Chatbot. Its testbed was an RTX 6000 server plus a supplementary M1 Pro MacBook,
   so "no RTX-laptop power sharing, no game foreground" holds. The paper argues "there is a need for SLO-aware scheduling" and does not implement one; its stated
   future work is edge-cloud speculative decoding, so "lists SLO-aware scheduling as open" is a fair reading of a need, not of a future-work item.
   The G-Assist frame-rate claim cites a news article that I did not open; unverified.

- Verdict (doc): no reading above 150 W exists in any stored record, and no headline number moves. The registered power figures come from the energy counter, not from the glitching call. Nothing was changed.
- The glitch is real and not caused by the project's code: nvidia-smi printed 588 to 590 W on an idle GPU (7 of 10 reads); 133 of 200 direct NVML reads at 20 Hz were above 150 W. Hypothesis, not tested: the instantaneous power field is bad while the GPU is idle. Not tested under load.
- Stored records scanned (all of data/, read-only): 0 readings above 150 W in any source; the largest W5b trace bin is 103.9 W.
- Not excluded: a glitch shorter than one NVML update inside the W5b cells, because raw samples were not stored there. Effect by arithmetic: about 0.03 W on a 60 s cell mean per sample.
- Phantom energy bound: 0.25 W worst case (both window reads at 7.5 J each over 60 s). At 0.25 W, 2 feasibility flips and 3 of 383 registered outputs move (S0 non-inferiority vs c'B-T3b, HW1 for c'B-T3b in S0, HW1 for eB in S1 at 35 W). At 0.125 W none move. The d'B verdict ("does not earn its place") stands under both readings.
- Correction recorded in the doc: the first addendum version re-ran only the 60 W checks and was superseded by the full pass (commit 5274d83).
- Recommended guard (doc; the doc does not say it is implemented): reject readings above 1.5 times the device power limit, store a per-bin maximum, and apply the guard before the Governor's recent mean.

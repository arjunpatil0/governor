Stall check (report section "Stall check": first three cells after each stall vs the same cell in the other 9 blocks): mean/p99 latency, completed fraction and clocks lie inside the other blocks' ranges; **board power is below all 9 other blocks in 5 of the 6 cells** (e.g. 41.0 W vs 44.1 W median for b07_08, 48.9 vs 52.9 for b08_16; the sixth is rank 1 of 9), and the pre-cell GPU temperature was 64 C (others 58-68 C). I do not claim a mechanism (a cooler chip is one candidate, untested). Power is not a variable of any A2 test, so the stalls do not affect the A2 results reported; they would matter for any power analysis.

- **SA4** (mean latency / fg-alone latency; completed fraction alongside): fg 1 ms alone 1.118 ms; with bg m=2 / m=8 slowdown 2.65 / 2.72, all completed. fg 4 ms, duty 0.25 / 0.5 / 0.75 / 1.0 (m=2): slowdown 1.36 / 1.67 / 2.13 / 2.91 vs the 1 + d reference 1.25 / 1.5 / 1.75 / 2.0 (m=8, d=1: 3.08); all completed. fg 9 ms (the pilot frame, ~12.2 ms alone at 1800 MHz): duty 0.25 slowdown 1.17 (reference 1.25), all completed; **duty >= 0.5 and d = 1 are OVERLOADED**: duty 0.5 mean latency 358 ms although the completed fraction is 1.0 (a backlog), duty 0.75 completed 0.77, d = 1 completed 0.54 (m=2) / 0.49 (m=8). The nominal 9 ms stream cannot be compared at high duty; this was foreseeable (journal 0021).
- **Exploratory capped-wait test (addendum, post-hoc)**: fitted tau = **0.260 ms**, 90% block-bootstrap CI [0.260, 0.270], offset 0.041 ms, on A's SA1 cells (59,985 requests); per m: 0.260 / 0.270 / 0.270 (m = 2 / 4 / 8). A's median-based estimate was 0.308 ms (a different six-point estimator without a CI). The high-priority A2 cells (a different condition) give tau 0.260 [0.260, 0.270]. The grid step is 0.01 ms, so CIs narrower than that are grid-limited. **Caveat that must stay attached: the SA1 cells are A's own cells, the data that suggested tau, so this is a request-level re-description of A, not an independent test. The addendum text implied A2 data would test the hypothesis; that was my error when writing it, because the registered SA1 cells are A cells.** The binned curve rises from 0.07 ms at 0-0.05 ms time-to-slice-end to about 0.34-0.44 ms by 0.3-0.4 ms, then is not flat: it falls to 0.154 ms at 0.60-0.65 ms and climbs back to about 0.35 ms by 1.0 ms, after which it stays around 0.28-0.34 ms out to 9 ms. The capped-wait model does not describe that dip and rise; no explanation is offered.

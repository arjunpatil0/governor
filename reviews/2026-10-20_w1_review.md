Reviewer: Claude (cloud session, research reviewer). Reviewed at git HEAD fe6eb72 (tree dirty).
Status key: [BLOCKER] fix before the full w1_downclock run · [FIX] fix soon · [NOTE] no action needed

## Overall
Solid work. The audit is thorough, the pre-registration is well designed (blocked pairing, Holm,
validity checks, decision rule fixed in advance), and execution mode/provenance is logged. Kernels look valid:
mem ≈ 232 GB/s (~90% of the ~256 GB/s peak at 8001 MHz on a 128-bit bus), fma ≈ 18.6 TFLOP/s
(~80% of 36 SMs × 128 lanes × 2 × 2.475 GHz ≈ 22.8 TFLOP/s). So "memory-bound" and "compute-bound" really are
what the names say. Peak figures are back-of-envelope; state them as such if they go in any doc.

## Findings
1. [BLOCKER] Energy counter vs 20 Hz power samples disagree by 20–50% in the smoke runs
   (e.g. 89.1 W vs 59.2 W, 60.1 vs 46.5). At the full run's 15% threshold almost every cell will be flagged.
   Before the full run, find out why. Likely causes to check, in order:
   (a) sample window misaligned with the measured window (samples include idle/preroll time, or the kernel
       ends before the 1 s window does: gpu_ms ≈ 1018 vs wall_s 1.02);
   (b) the NVML power reading is a slow moving average (power updates median 22 ms but p90 500 ms in the audit),
       so 20 Hz sampling over 1 s mostly sees stale/lagging values;
   (c) a units or field mismatch (instant vs average power field).
   Write a short diagnostic: a 10 s constant fma load, logging energy-counter deltas every 100 ms next to power
   samples, then plot both. The energy counter is the pre-registered primary source, so if (b) is the cause,
   record that the samples check is unreliable as a validity check, in a new file (w1_downclock_v2.yaml
   or an addendum). Do NOT edit the registered yaml.
2. [FIX] temp_start_c > temp_max_c in mem rows (61 vs 53, 58 vs 53). Max over the window can't be below the start.
   Either temp_max excludes the start sample or a stale/cached reading is used. Fix and add a unit test.
3. [FIX] event_reasons shows "Reliability" under full load, while the audit showed ['SwPowerCap'] under matmul.
   Check the bitmask decoding against the NVML constants (nvmlClocksEventReason*). A wrong decoder would mislabel
   every throttle state in W2.
4. [FIX] Smoke meta shows git_dirty: true. The full run must happen on a clean, committed tree (yaml_sha +
   git_head are only meaningful if clean). Make run.py refuse to start a non-smoke run when dirty.
5. [NOTE] Mem kernel draws ~57–61 W unlocked vs ~87–93 W for fma at the same 2475 MHz. That already points the way
   H2 predicts. Don't report it as a result; it's smoke data.
6. [NOTE] Energy Meter (CPU) is still unvalidated. Not needed for w1_downclock (GPU only), but needed before any
   CPU-power claim in W2. Validate against HWiNFO once Arjun installs it.
7. [NOTE] PDH not attributing CUDA compute is an important finding: per-process GPU split must come from our own
   instrumentation (CUDA events in our bg/fg workloads), not from the OS. Record in CLAUDE.md under decisions.

## 2. FINDING: the governed (c'B) cells' power and tokens are not credible; the c'B grid is VOID
All 36 c'B cells report 63-106 W (mean about 90 W) with 0-10 tok/s and temp_after 59-63 C. Same-activity statics: 1800_a_g0_d1 (3B, S1) 42.0 W at 20 tok/s; unlocked_a_g0_d1 (3B, S0) 68.9 W at 35.8 tok/s; the c'B 3B S0 cells make 4.7 tok/s at 92-106 W, Phi S0 cells make 0 tok/s at 69-92 W where the Phi statics draw 30 W. A 90 W load for 60 s heats the card to 80 C (arm a: 83 C); the c'B cells end at 60 C. The first trial's governed cells showed the same (trial_cb 81.9 W at 2.8 tok/s, trial_db 97.9 W at 1.2 tok/s), so it is not a late-sitting drift. The simulator does not reproduce it. Two candidate causes, both on the governed path only: (a) the energy-counter power (cell metric AND the Governor's own power signal) is inflated, which would also make the controllers' power loops throttle the background to almost nothing - consistent with the tiny tok/s; (b) real extra load. A transient nvidia-smi reading of 590 W at idle after the sitting (17 W a few minutes later) shows the power telemetry can glitch. The clock log of the sitting also shows c'B clock flip-flops at cell start (1500-1350-1500-1800 within 4 s).
Consequences: (1) the c'B constants of decisions.json (rule_met False) are void and are NOT used; (2) the c'B grid is re-run in block T3b under the final controller; (3) NO T3b before a diagnostic A/B: every hardware cell now logs a second, independent power measure (the 250 Hz NVML power sample mean, `power_sampled_w`) next to the energy counter and the morning check flags a disagreement above 15%; `tune.py --diag` runs 4 cells: the g'B action through the static path (diag_static), through the Governor loop (diag_gov_static), through the Governor loop without its 10 Hz NVML energy reads (diag_gov_static_noenergy), and c'B (diag_cb). Gate for T3b: diag_gov_static power within 15% of diag_static AND every diag cell's energy power within 15% of its sampled power; otherwise T3b does not start and the anomaly is investigated first.

## 3. c'B: token-aware switching threshold (reviewer; supersedes the fixed 6.57 ms of addendum O-2 section 1)
Unaligned when the recent measured frame time exceeds threshold + 0.3 ms, aligned below threshold - 0.3 ms, dwell 5 s (unchanged), with threshold = period - the CLEAN token duration (never below 0): 6.57 ms for the 3B (tonight's 3B behaviour is unchanged), about 12.2 ms for the 1B, below 0 for tokens longer than the period (the 8B in S5 never goes aligned). The clean token duration is the median of the last 20 token durations seen while c'B is aligned (tokens run in the slack, alone) or before any frame arrived (the startup tokens, the background alone); dilated tokens of the unaligned mode never enter; before any token the L-table prior at the current clock is used. NOTE: the reviewer named the driver's est_clean_ms; it is not in the token ring (only the driver CSV), and changing the ring would change the driver executable (hash bb875fbc must stay), so the controller uses the same quantity from the ring (median of clean token durations). Tests: tests/test_pi_switch.py (12). The sitting's 1B and Phi c'B cells used the fixed 6.57 ms and are void anyway.

# ADDENDUM O — d'B fallback confirmed, HGB finding, d'B safety margin, exploratory arm d'B-HGB, tuning-block procedure (written 2026-10-23, BEFORE any W5b data)
Sources: reviews/2026-10-23_w5a_selection2_review.md and Arjun's decisions of 2026-10-23 (1)-(4). The registered yaml is not edited; this addendum is the record and extends it where the yaml says "tuned on the tuning blocks" without a rule.

## 1. Decisions recorded
(1) The registered fallback of addendum N applies: d'B in W5b = the hybrid (idle branch = eB rule; active branch = the grey-box ModelController v2+v3) with the safety shield unchanged. (2) FINDING (post-hoc re-run, descriptive, not a confirmed result): on W5a data the learned residual model HGB predicted feasibility far more safely than the physics-based grey-box: false-feasible cells 1 (0.8%, 95% CI 0-4.6%) vs 39 (22.5%); unsafe picks 0 of 8 vs 4 of 10; lowest regret (0.18); power MAE 2.4 W; it missed the tok/s coverage gate by 1.8 points (88.2% vs 90%), driven by the 1B fold (78%; the 1B is 6-14x faster than the other workloads). LIMITATION: the interval method lacked a per-workload throughput scale. Under the rule fixed in advance HGB was NOT adopted. No candidate made an informative pick on c14, the heaviest condition.

## 2. d'B feasibility safety margin (a tuned constant; no W5a number sets it)
The W5a result motivates it (the grey-box is optimistic about feasibility). The margin m is the fraction of the deadline held back from the model's predicted q95 frame time: m = 1 - `safety` of controllers/model.py (ModelController admits a plan only if predicted q95 <= safety x deadline). Grid m in {0.0, 0.1, 0.2, 0.3, 0.4} (safety 1.0, 0.9, 0.8, 0.7, 0.6). RULE (pre-stated): m* = the SMALLEST m for which the mean miss fraction over ALL d'B tuning cells run at that m (3 known workloads x scenes S0 and S1 at 60 W = 6 cells, at the chosen c0) is <= 1%. If no m satisfies it, m* = 0.4 and the report says the rule was not met. Tuned on T1-T3 known-workload data only; the held-out 8B never touches it. The same m* is used at 35 W. All other d'B constants stay at their sim-tuned values (data/sim_tuned.json: switch_gain 2.0, clock_every 20) and the ModelController defaults (guard_decay 0.02 etc.); they are not tuned.

## 3. Exploratory arm d'B-HGB (registered now, outside every confirmatory family)
Design: the same hybrid (idle branch = eB rule, same shield, same hand-over) with HGB as the active-branch predictor (the registered HGB of addendum L section 4, trained on the 280 W5a cells only, fixed after training; online calibration of the grey-box scalars in the loop), margin tuned by the SAME rule as section 2 on T1-T3 data. Staging (declared now): its controller is written test-first AFTER the T1-T3 sitting (it needs the tuned c0 and the shield constants) and its margin is tuned in a block T3b (the same 6-cell rule, 5 margins = 30 cells) before evaluation block 4. Evaluated only in scenes S1 and S5 at 60 W in evaluation blocks 4-8 (10 extra cells, about 12 min); it is in NO Holm family and NO confirmatory test (HW2, HW3 do not include it), and is reported descriptively as "learning in general vs the weaker predictor the fallback forced". If it is not implemented, tested and tuned before block 4 it is declared DROPPED in the journal and no d'B-HGB cell is run.

## 4. Tuning blocks T1-T3: procedure and decision rules (first time stated; written before any W5b data)
Cells: 5 s warm-up + 60 s measured (yaml), foreground = synthetic FMA at nominal cost c0 times the scene factor (S0 = 1, S1 = steps 1.0/0.7/1.3/0.7/1.0/1.3 every 10 s), background = the governed driver of the cell's workload; 20 s minimum cool-down (and < 55 C) between cells, 10 s unlocked idle first; cell order inside a batch is seeded-random (seed 20261024). Feasible cell: miss fraction INCLUDING abandoned frames <= 1%, mean power <= budget, and tok/s >= 20% of the background-alone tok/s of the same workload. Budgets 60 W (primary) and 35 W (exploratory); statics are run once and judged against both.
T1: (a) one DISCARDED full cell (arm a, 3B, S0), (b) background-alone cell (unlocked, no foreground, 30 s measured) for each of llama32_1b, llama32_3b, phi35_mini, (c) c0 search in the pre-listed order 7, 8, 9, 10, 11 ms nominal on the 3B: a foreground-alone calibration cell (c0m = p50 latency, unlocked, 15 s), arm a in S1 (unlocked clock, unaligned, duty 1) - if its miss fraction is <= 5% the c0 fails the gate and the next c0 is tried; otherwise the T2 sweep below is run at this c0 and HW0 is evaluated at 60 W: the smallest c0 with a feasible static is the chosen c0 (35 W is reported, not used). No feasible c0: the run STOPS and the result is reported (no other c0 is invented).
T2: the 70 statics (7 clocks x [aligned guard {0, 1} x duty {0.25, 0.5, 1} + unaligned duty {0.1, 0.25, 0.5, 1}]) on the 3B in S1 at the c0 under test; the sweep at the chosen c0 is the T2 block (sweeps at failed c0 stay in the data as T1).
T3 stage 2: per budget the 8 best FEASIBLE stage-1 settings by tok/s (all of them if fewer than 8), the union over both budgets run on 1B, 3B and Phi in S0 and S1 (cells already run are not repeated). g'B per budget = the setting feasible in all 6 (workload x scene) cells with the highest mean tok/s; if none, the setting with the most feasible cells, ties by mean tok/s (reported); eB = g'B plus the idle drop (no separate choice).
c'B: pacing mode fixed by c0m (measured at the chosen c0): aligned if c0m <= 6.57 ms, else unaligned; constants: grid target {0.5, 0.65, 0.8} x ki {0.05, 0.15}, guard0 0.5, gmin 0.3, other PIController defaults, 60 W cells on the 3 workloads x {S0, S1} (36 cells); choice = the combination with the highest mean tok/s among those with mean miss <= 1% and mean power <= 60 W, else the lowest mean miss. The same constants are used at 35 W with the 35 W budget.
d'B: section 2 (5 margins x 3 workloads x {S0, S1} = 30 cells at 60 W).
Size: at most about 330 cells (estimate; the measured cell time replaces it); the cell counts per stage are logged. SITTING: T1-T3 are CHAINED in one unattended sitting (the yaml's "sessions of at most 3 h, a session ends only between blocks" is relaxed for the tuning blocks only, because they are not in any confirmatory test and a restart cannot be automated; reason, start and end time and the uptime at start are recorded in meta.json; evaluation blocks keep the 3 h rule and a fresh restart each). The sitting starts only if uptime <= 2 h (addendum M), AC power, Armoury mode stated, Chrome closed, clean committed tree, identical configuration throughout.
Abort/flag rules: those of the yaml; additionally the sitting stops after a failure that repeats on two consecutive cells (the cause is logged), and every stop is resumable (finished cells are kept; the decisions are recomputed deterministically from the stored cells).
Everything in this section is a development step: no tuning-block number enters a confirmatory test.

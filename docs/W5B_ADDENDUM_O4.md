## 1. Hypothesis, prediction, decision rule (finding 1)
HYPOTHESIS: the governed-cell power of the T1-T3 sitting is NVML energy-counter phantom energy. The Governor loop calls nvmlDeviceGetTotalEnergyConsumption once per tick; journal 0005 measured about 4.2 J of phantom energy per extra read. MEASURED READ RATE: in the 36 c'B cells of the sitting the Governor ticked 10.01-10.02 times per second (frames.npz gov_log; mean 10.02), i.e. 10.0 energy reads/s, so the predicted inflation is 4.2 J x 10.0/s = about +42 W on the counter; statics (30-70 W) plus 42 W give 72-112 W, the observed range of the governed cells is 63-106 W (mean 90.5 W) at 59-63 C. This is a consistency check made after the data, not the test; the test is the diagnostic below.
The Governor loop reading is also the controllers' power signal (Obs.power_w), so a phantom 40 W would make the power loops throttle the background: consistent with 0-10 tok/s.
DIAGNOSTIC ARMS (tune.py --diag, 3B, S1, c0 = 7, the g'B action 1800_a_g0_d1 for the first three): diag_static (static path, counter read twice); diag_gov_static (Governor loop WITH its energy reads); diag_gov_static_noenergy (Governor loop WITHOUT energy reads); diag_cb (c'B, with reads); diag_cb_sampled (c'B with the power source switched to the mean of the NVML power samples: the fix). Every cell logs the counter power E, the independent sampled mean M (250 Hz GetPowerUsage), the Governor's energy reads per second r, tok/s.
PREDICTION (a), tested by the gate: (i) on the arms with reads, E - M = 4.2 J x r (+-35% or +-5 W, whichever is larger), and E(diag_gov_static) - E(diag_static) = the same; (ii) the no-reads arm matches the static arm within 15% (counter power); (iii) the sampled mean is not inflated: diag_gov_static and the no-reads arm within 15% of the static arm's M (c'B arms excepted: a controller may change the load); (iv) the fix arm diag_cb_sampled has E within 15% of its M.
DECISION RULE (b), automatic: CONFIRMED_PHANTOM (all of (i)-(iv)) -> the Governor loop's power signal is the sampled source, T3b runs with it (Governor.step(power_w=...), HwExecutor power_source 'sampled': no energy reads in the loop, only the two window reads; its ~1.5 s NVML lag is acceptable for the slow loop; the power source is recorded in meta.json and in every governed cell). NO_ANOMALY (E agrees with M on all five arms and the Governor arm matches the static arm) -> source 'energy', T3b runs as designed. ANYTHING ELSE (the no-reads arm is also off, the sampled mean is inflated, the inflation does not match 4.2 J x r, the fix arm disagrees) -> STOP: a new problem, T3b does not start, the output is the result of the night. The script reads the verdict from power_source.json; `tune.py --plan t3b` REFUSES to start without a usable verdict.
GATE PRINTOUT (c): for all five arms E, M, E - M, r and tok/s; the predicted inflation (4.2 J x r) next to the observed (governor arm - static arm, and counter - sampled), the same for the c'B arm; the verdict and the reasons.
The "sampled" source is also the right one for the evaluation blocks if confirmed; this is recorded now so that it is not a post-hoc choice: any governed evaluation cell uses the source the gate selected, written into meta.json.

## 3. Infeasible comparators in the registered analysis (finding 5): confirmed in writing, nothing changed
The registration (experiments/w5_governor.yaml HW2) and the committed analysis (analysis/w5_analysis.py, tested before any data) already decide this: the SCORE of a cell is its tok/s if the cell is feasible (miss <= 1% and mean power <= budget; in evaluation there is no 20%-of-background-alone condition, that exists only in the tuning selection) and 0 otherwise; infeasible cells are NOT dropped. A comparator that admits no token (g'B / eB aligned at c0 = 7 for the 3B in S0) therefore scores 0. SUPERIORITY: paired difference d'B - comparator with the 0 counted; blocks where both score 0 are ties and drop out; fewer than 6 non-tied blocks = INCONCLUSIVE. NON-INFERIORITY (S0, S2): blocks where the comparator scores 0 are EXCLUDED from the ratio and their number reported (nothing to be inferior to); if the comparator scores 0 in EVERY block the test passes VACUOUSLY (analysis/w5_analysis.py, `n = 0, pass True`). Added now, REPORTING ONLY (no pass/fail changes): the report must state, for every non-inferiority line, the number of usable blocks and mark a vacuous pass as "VACUOUS (comparator scored 0 in every block)"; a non-inferiority claim resting on a vacuous pass is not described as a result of the learned controller.

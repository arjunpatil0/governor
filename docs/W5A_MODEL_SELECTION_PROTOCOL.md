Registered after the reviewer's changes 1-3 and notes 5-6 (reviews/2026-10-23_w5a_model_selection_protocol_review.md) and Arjun's decisions (a) accepted with the design-based rule, (b) accepted plus changes 2-3, (c) scikit-learn HistGradientBoosting instead of LightGBM (NOT installed: a pip install could upgrade numpy/scipy in the middle of the study; same model family; substitution recorded here). ## 1. Data (facts, data/w5a/verification.json; counts only, no target values inspected)
280 cells, 3 workloads x 6 conditions x 15-16 of the 70 clock x pacing combinations; 80 overloaded cells (frames abandoned; `miss_frac_incl_abandoned` >= 0.5), 103 of the other 200 with zero tokens. Power exists once per 30 s cell. The 8B never appears.

## 2. Targets and scoring (per test cell; test cells exclude the adaptation cells)
T1 `miss` = cell.json `miss_frac_incl_abandoned` (never `miss_frac`). Reported: MAE; classification of "miss <= 0.01": FALSE-FEASIBLE rate (predicted <= 0.01, observed > 0.01; the one that matters) and FALSE-INFEASIBLE rate reported separately, each as a share of all test cells and of the cells of its true class.
T2 `tok_s`: MAE (tok/s); zero-token classification (predicted < 1 tok/s vs observed 0).
T3 `power_w`: MAE (W); classification at 60 W (the primary budget), false-feasible/false-infeasible as in T1.
T4 `p99` fg latency (`fg_p99_ms`), only non-overloaded cells (abandoned = 0): the p99 (99th percentile) of the absolute error, with the count excluded reported. MAE also reported.
Intervals: every candidate gives a 95% interval for T2-T4 (y_hat +/- 1.96 sigma_hat, tok_s lower bound clipped at 0). Coverage per target pooled over all test cells of all LOWO folds must lie in 90-98%.
Latency: predict + decide p99 < 1 ms on this CPU for the 70 settings (7 clocks x 10 pacing configurations) of one state (3B, condition c9), all four targets, intervals and the decision rule of section 5, measured as p99 over 300 repetitions after a warm-up of 50.

## 3. LOWO, design-based adaptation (finding 1), K = 3 primary, K = 1 sensitivity
Folds: hold out llama32_1b, llama32_3b, phi35_mini in turn (3 folds); per-fold results are reported next to the pooled ones (note 5), and the pooled result is never reported without them. Fit on the other two workloads; adapt on K cells of the held-out one; score on its remaining cells.
Adaptation cells of workload W (design fields only, never outcomes): eligible = cells of W with mode unaligned and duty >= 0.5 (pacing u_d0.5, u_d1), sorted by (session, order); take, in that order, the first cell whose clock label has not been taken yet, until K cells; if fewer than K distinct clock labels exist, fill with the next eligible cells. The K = 3 set contains the K = 1 set. The same rule picks each TRAINING workload's calibration cells. K = 1 is reported as sensitivity (not used for selection). The plan's "10 s" adaptation is replaced by K whole cells because power exists once per cell; the 10 s version is tested on hardware in the W5b tuning blocks.
Leave-one-condition-out (reported only, NOT part of the selection rule): hold out each of c5, c7, c9, c11, c14 and var9 (c14 is beyond the c0 search range, the S1 heavy-phase extrapolation case, note 6) with all three workloads; calibration cells are chosen by the same rule from the training conditions; no adaptation on the held-out condition.

## 4. Candidates (all predict T1-T4 from the same inputs; seeds fixed; deterministic)
Inputs x: effective clock (unlocked = 2460 MHz), mode (0/1), guard_ms, duty, nominal fg cost c (ms alone at 1800 MHz; var9 = 9), varying flag. All learned candidates also get the calibrated grey-box predictions and the workload descriptors (alpha, delta, and ln of the calibrated/prior token time at 900 and 1800 MHz) from the calibration below.
G (candidate 1, grey-box + RLS), analytic steady state, vectorised numpy, no fitted constants except the three calibrated scalars. P = 1000/60 ms, S = sim.params share divisor, tau0(f) = 1000/tok_s_L(f) (L table, 3B), fgP(f) fg power (W1), bgP(f) bg power (L), p_idle (journal 0011).
 F = alpha * c * fg_ratio(f), alpha prior 0.745 (smoke 3, journal 0043, indicative measured/nominal ratio). tau(f) = controllers.tokmodel.TokenTimeModel (batch, forget 1, quadratic in ln(f/1800)) calibrated clean token time.
 ALIGNED: slack W = P - F - guard; n = 0 if W < tau else floor((W - tau) * duty / tau) + 1 tokens per frame; tok_s = min(60 n, 1000 duty / tau); miss = 1 if F > P else 0; p99 = F; fg busy u = F/P; bg busy = tok_s tau / 1000.
 UNALIGNED: beta = duty; F_eff = F ((1 - beta) + beta S); overload if F_eff > P (miss = 1, p99 undefined); u = F_eff / P; tau_eff = tau ((1 - u) + u S); tok_s = 1000 beta / tau_eff; x* = (P - F) / (S - 1); p_miss = 0 if x* >= min(F, tau_eff) else beta (1 - x* / min(F, tau_eff)); p99 = F + (S - 1) min(F, tau_eff) if beta > 0.01 else F.
 POWER: time-weighted: p_idle + (u - o)(fgP - p_idle) + (b - o)(bgP + delta - p_idle) + o ((fgP + bgP + delta)/2 - p_idle), b = bg busy fraction, o = u b.
 var9: the six phases (cost x 1.0, 0.7, 1.3, 0.7, 1.0, 1.3) are evaluated separately; miss, tok_s, power = mean over phases, p99 = max over phases.
 CALIBRATION from the adaptation cells in order: tau-curve (TokenTimeModel.update with observed tok_ms_p50 divided by the contention dilation computed with alpha prior; skipped when the cell has no tokens), then alpha (exp of the mean of ln(fg_p50_obs / fg_p50_pred), pred = F (1 + (S-1) beta), shrunk with prior weight 1; skipped when the cell is overloaded), then delta (mean of power_obs - power_pred, shrunk n/(n+1)). Interval sigma_hat = pooled residual std of G on the TRAINING workloads' test cells after the same calibration with their own K cells.
HGB (candidate 2): scikit-learn HistGradientBoostingRegressor (max_depth 3, max_iter 100, learning_rate 0.1, min_samples_leaf 10, random_state 0) on the RESIDUAL y - G_cal, per target, trained on the training workloads; online residual = shrunk mean (n/(n+1)) of the remaining residual on the K adaptation cells. LightGBM's monotone constraints are not used (HGB: none).
GP (candidate 3): scikit-learn GaussianProcessRegressor on standardised inputs and residual, kernel Constant x Matern(nu 2.5, ARD) + White, a random subset of at most 150 training cells (seed 0) = subset-of-data sparse approximation (not the inducing-point form), same online residual.
MLP (candidate 4): 5 x MLPRegressor(32, 32), alpha 1e-3, max_iter 1500, seeds 0-4, standardised inputs and residual, mean of the 5, same online residual.
Intervals for HGB/GP/MLP: y_hat +/- 1.96 sigma_hat, sigma_hat = pooled out-of-fold residual std from INNER leave-one-workload-out folds on the training workloads (same candidate, same calibration). GP predictive std and ensemble spread are NOT used (deviation from plan B, for a uniform, checkable calibration). The CUSUM change detector is an online-loop component with no cell-level meaning; it is not evaluated here (W5b).
Simplicity order: G < HGB < GP < MLP.

## 5. Decision metrics (findings 2-3)
For each held-out stratum (workload x condition) the model picks among that stratum's test cells: predicted-feasible = y_miss <= 0.01 and y_power + 1.96 sigma_power <= 60; pick = argmax predicted tok_s among predicted-feasible with predicted tok_s >= 1, else no pick. Observed-feasible = miss_frac_incl_abandoned <= 0.01 and power_w <= 60; best = max observed tok_s among observed-feasible test cells of the stratum (0 if none or < 1 tok/s).
REGRET = best - observed tok_s of the pick (the full best if the pick is observed-infeasible; the full best if there is no pick; 0 if best = 0). Reported absolute (tok/s) and as REGRET_REL = sum regret / sum best over strata with best > 0.
FALSE-FEASIBLE RATE = share of picks that are observed-infeasible (also reported: picks made, strata with best > 0).

## 6. Selection rule (fixed)
Hard gates (K = 3, pooled LOWO): G1 false-feasible rate of picks <= 5%; G2 coverage 90-98% for T2, T3, T4; G3 latency p99 < 1 ms. Among candidates passing G1-G3: let best_e be the smallest value among them of each of p99-error (T4), tok_s MAE, power MAE; a candidate QUALIFIES if each of those three is <= 1.10 best_e and REGRET_REL <= min REGRET_REL + 0.10 (absolute; a ratio rule would be degenerate near 0). Selected = the simplest qualifying candidate (order in section 4). If no candidate passes G1-G3, nothing is selected and the report says which gate failed; no fallback is chosen in advance. If G qualifies, the report says the learned part is only the online calibration.

## 7. Not done here
No constants of d'B (guard growth, thresholds) are tuned on W5a; no claim about the 10 s adaptation, the 35 W budget or the held-out 8B; simulator results are not used.
